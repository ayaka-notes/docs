The following pages provide detailed descriptions of the various configuration files and settings that are required to configure and customize your deployment when you deploy with Docker Compose through the Ayakaleaf Toolkit.

Each of these files plays a specific role in the configuration process, and understanding their functions will help you to tailor your on-premises version of Overleaf to your specific needs.
