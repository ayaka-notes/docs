An account can have more than one email. Add one when an address will be taken away, such as a university's after you graduate, so that you keep your account and your projects.

The list of emails appears when your instance can send email. Otherwise the settings page has a single email field.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your emails">
Click **Account**, then **Account settings**. Your emails are under **Emails and affiliations**.

<Frame caption="Your emails">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-list.png" alt="The Emails and affiliations section with one primary email and the link Add another email" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Add the new email">
Click **Add another email**, type the address and click **Add new email**.

<Frame caption="Adding an email">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-add.png" alt="The form with a new address and the button Add new email" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the code">
A 6-digit code is sent to the new address. Enter it and click **Confirm**. If nothing arrives, click **Resend confirmation code**.

<Frame caption="Confirming the new email">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-code.png" alt="The form Confirm your email address asking for the 6-digit code" />
</Frame>

The email is now in your list, and your primary email gets a message that it was added.

<Frame caption="The new email in the list">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-added.png" alt="The list with the primary email and the new email with a Make primary button" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Make it your primary email">
Click **Make primary** next to the new email, then **Change primary email**.

<Frame caption="Confirming the change">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-make-primary.png" alt="The dialog Confirm primary email change with the button Change primary email" />
</Frame>

From now on, log in with the new email. The old one stays in the list.

<Frame caption="The new primary email">
  <img src="/images/on-premises/multiple-emails-primary-changed.png" alt="The list with the new email as primary and the old email below it" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

## What each email can do

* Only the primary email logs in and gets password resets. A secondary email does neither, so an address that is later given to someone else cannot take over your account.
* Press **Make primary** before the old mailbox closes. Afterwards only an administrator can change your email.
* An old primary email that you confirmed stays as a secondary email. One that was never confirmed is removed when another email becomes primary.
* To remove a secondary email, click its trash icon. Another account can then add that address.
* An address that belongs to another account cannot be added.
* On an instance with LDAP or SAML login you can add emails, but **Make primary** is refused, because you could no longer log in with the new email.
