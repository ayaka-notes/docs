### Creating your first administrator account

In a browser, open [http://localhost/launchpad](http://localhost/launchpad). You should see a form with email and password fields. Fill these in with the credentials you want to use as the administrator account, then click **Register**.

<Info>

Passwords must be at least 8 characters long (no more than 72), cannot contain email addresses or be too similar to email addresses, and must contain only valid characters.

</Info>

![Overleaf Launchpad](/images/on-premises/image.jpg)

Then click the link to go to the login page ([http://localhost/login](http://localhost/login)). Enter the credentials. Once you're logged in, you'll see the welcome page.

![Overleaf welcome page](/images/on-premises/image-1.jpg)

Click the green button at the bottom of the page to start using Overleaf.

### Creating your first example project

Open [http://localhost/project](http://localhost/project) in your browser and you will see a button prompting you to create your first project.

![Welcome to Overleaf!](/images/on-premises/image-2.jpg)

Click this button, select <strong>Example Project,</strong> and follow the on-screen instructions.

You will be taken to the new project, where you will see a text editor and a PDF preview.

![Overleaf Editor](/images/on-premises/image-3.jpg)

To finish you can click on the **Home** icon to return to your projects page.
